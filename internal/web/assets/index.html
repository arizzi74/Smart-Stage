BACKGROUND & TRANSITIONS

Stage & sound

Add an image or video to the playlist, then select it here. Background videos loop. Background buttons can change this selection for the current session.

Applies to every background video while stage is on. A music or video cue replaces its sound; STOP returns to the background soundtrack.

Starting from silence plays immediately. When sound is already playing, crossfade to the next sound. STOP fades to silence or the background soundtrack.

Stopping music this way keeps the displayed image. Stage on and off leave music playing.

HOST STATUS

Status

Escape immediately silences all sound and closes the stage. Quit Smart Stage exits the app.

Trusted LAN only

HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Pairing protects control access, but cannot protect against someone listening on the network. Keep the host and controllers on a trusted network.
